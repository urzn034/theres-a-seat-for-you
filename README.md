# There's a Seat for You

A 2D pixel-art cafe game made in Unity. You play as Eve, a barista at Finch's Cafe in the quiet village of Willowmere.

> **Note:** This is a short demo of a larger concept. The game currently covers Day 1 and Day 2 only, and the full story is still in development.

Made for the LPPC Gunadarma lab assistant selection.

## Synopsis

Eve is a former graffiti artist with sage-colored hair who now works the counter at Finch's Cafe, owned by Mr. Barnaby Finch. Every day, the villagers of Willowmere drop by, each carrying a story along with their order. Eve listens, brews, and serves, and slowly the cafe becomes a place where everyone has a seat.

In this demo:
- **Day 1:** Theo Baker orders a Black Coffee.
- **Day 2:** Mrs. Marsh orders a Milk Coffee.

## About the Title

*"There's a Seat for You"* is the core idea of the game. The cafe is more than a place to drink coffee: it is a place where anyone can sit down, be heard, and feel welcome. The title is still a working concept and may change as the full game develops.

## How to Play

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / Arrow keys | Move |
| E | Interact / pick up |
| Q | Drop held item |
| B | Open / close recipe book |
| SPACE | Hit the golden zone in the brewing mini-game |

**Loop:** take an order at the counter, grab ingredients, put them in the espresso machine, press E when "Tekan E" appears, hit SPACE in the golden zone, then bring the drink to the customer's table.

**Recipes**
- Black Coffee: Coffee Beans + Hot Water
- Honey Tea: Tea Leaves + Hot Water + Honey
- Milk Coffee (Day 2): Coffee Beans + Milk + Cinnamon

## Run the Game

1. Go to the **Releases** page and download `Build.zip`
2. Unzip it
3. Run `Pixel Project.exe`

## Open in Unity

- Unity version: **2022.3.62f2** (Universal 2D)
- Clone this repo, then in Unity Hub choose **Add** and select the cloned folder
- The first open takes a while because Unity rebuilds the Library folder
- Open the `MainMenu` scene in `Assets/Scenes` and press Play
